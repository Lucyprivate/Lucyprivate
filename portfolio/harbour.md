# Harbour Atlas

**Hong Kong's photographic city surface with visible source records.**

![Hong Kong harbour overview](media/harbour-overview.jpg)

[Watch the 60-second film](media/harbour.mp4)

## Goal and source

The study presents the Lands Department's official 3D Visualisation Map.
It retains source texture coordinates and photographic appearance.
The source collection contains **2,302 tiles**.
The presentation uses **2,197 tiles** and **2,240 original texture images**.
It separates detailed landmark regions from coarser context.

![Wan Chai photographic city view](media/harbour-wan-chai.jpg)

## Method and limits

A derivative of the official mapped water forms a continuous display surface.
The raw-source mode retains the original photographic water.
The selected water height is a display parameter, rather than a measured tide.

The photographic source does not establish its vertical datum.
The terrain has a known Hong Kong Principal Datum (HKPD) reference.
The display keeps that terrain separate because the sources do not establish a common height datum.
Mixed revision dates do not establish each photograph's capture date or a present-day survey.

## Results

Local inspection examined browser views, source picking, and display modes.
A portable Rhino model visibly loaded its adjacent textures.
The film uses an actual **1,440-frame browser capture**.
The review decoded the full film and examined selected frames.
Texture seams, reconstruction roughness, and different levels of detail remain visible.
