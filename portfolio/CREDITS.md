## Hong Kong Urban Site Study

The **[Hong Kong Urban Site Study](https://github.com/Lucyprivate/hong-kong-urban-site-study)** develops shared source materials.
It uses geographic information systems (GIS) and Rhino.
Ewan set the brief, supplied resources, and directed revisions.
The project combines data processing, scripts, model integration, automated checks, and documentation.
Shared inputs remain collaborative work.

The current profile image is a **local Rhino 8.6 viewport capture from 14 October 2026**.
The **3,840 × 1,968** image shows an urban river district, rather than the full site.
The [model and preview notes](https://github.com/Lucyprivate/hong-kong-urban-site-study/blob/main/docs/model-and-preview.md) record its source and capture method.

The earlier overview and detail images date from 9 October 2026.
Rhino exported them directly with ViewCaptureToFile at **2,400 × 1,230 pixels**.
They use hand-selected views in Line drawing style, without later image edits.
The source model did not receive saved changes.
These presentation images are separate from the 8 October benchmark image and the 24 September native Rhino images.

The current model retains black building attributes, gray terrain, and blue water across 100 one-kilometre blocks.
The model refinement retains saved render caches and bounded geometry changes.

Transport elevations, supports, and local terrain adjustments include estimates.
Sources include the Lands Department and Civil Engineering and Development Department, HKSAR Government.
The project's [source register](https://github.com/Lucyprivate/hong-kong-urban-site-study/blob/main/SOURCES.md) lists these departments and other providers.
The [validation notes](https://github.com/Lucyprivate/hong-kong-urban-site-study/blob/main/docs/validation.md) separate geometric checks from survey accuracy and unresolved height relationships.
The [performance results](https://github.com/Lucyprivate/hong-kong-urban-site-study/blob/main/docs/model-performance.md) compare specific local models and settings in small Rhino 8 samples.
Opening remained slower. Models and raw source data remain local.
